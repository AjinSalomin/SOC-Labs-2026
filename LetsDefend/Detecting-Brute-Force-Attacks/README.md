# Detecting Brute Force Attacks

## Overview
This lab focuses on understanding and detecting brute force attacks, a common technique used by attackers to gain unauthorized access to user accounts by repeatedly attempting different passwords.

Through this hands-on exercise, I learned how to recognize suspicious login activity, identify repeated authentication failures, and understand how brute force attempts can be detected during security monitoring.

## Objectives
* Understand how brute force attacks work.
* Identify repeated failed login attempts.
* Recognize suspicious authentication patterns.
* Understand how security analysts investigate potential brute force activity.
* Learn the importance of monitoring login events to detect unauthorized access attempts.

## Key Learnings
During this lab, I developed a better understanding of how brute force attacks appear in security logs and why authentication monitoring is important in a Security Operations Center (SOC).

Key takeaways include:

* Failed Login Monitoring: Understanding why repeated authentication failures can indicate a potential brute force attack.
* Suspicious Activity Detection: Recognizing unusual login patterns that may require further investigation.
* Account Security: Understanding the risks of weak passwords and repeated password-guessing attempts.
* SOC Investigation: Learning how analysts assess authentication events to determine whether activity is suspicious.
* Incident Detection: Understanding the importance of identifying potential attacks early to reduce the risk of unauthorized access.

## Skills Practiced
* Brute Force Attack Detection
* Authentication Log Analysis
* Failed Login Monitoring
* Suspicious Activity Identification
* Security Event Investigation
* SOC Alert Triage
